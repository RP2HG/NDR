Author: Russell

Russell¶

Despite attempts using AI to make the photo of me look like some sort of secret agent, my career has spanned well over 40 years in and around computing. Starting at a time when it was called Computing, I have worked in Information Technology aka "IT", battled with the Nexus of Forces, and emerged blinking into the light of Digitalisation. EVen now as I write this during summer 2026, the emergence of AI is changing things once again.

I am certainly old enough now that whenever some bright young thing tries to show me something amazing, I can say "Oh we've been able to do that for years ... Nothing New".

Over that time, I started as a programmer, then moved to Product Management, before spending the last 25 years as variously a Solution Architect, an Enterprise Architect, and latterly focussing on Business Architecture. The piece about Purpose of an IT Architect is based upon a myriad of conversations arising from this time.

Academically I have a BSc in Computer Science, an MBA, and an MSC in Systems Thinking in Practice. However despite attending many courses, and passing exams, I am very much in favour of praxis as part of my daily practice.

Hopefully this site contains aspects drawn from a wide variety of experiences. At the top of this site you'll find links to ways to contact me.
