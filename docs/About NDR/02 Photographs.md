## Photographs

While I infrequently use images to lighten pages, where I do unless specifically tagged all images are my own. When I remember I shoot with a Panasonic Lumix GX7, otherwise photographs have been captured using my iPhone with either the default camera shipped by Apple, or ProCamera when I am able to master the settings and make it work for me.

At the moment I am using [Affinity](https://www.affinity.studio/) on my laptop as the image editor.

For many years I have used [Paint.NET](https://paint.net/#google_vignette) which is an excellent image editor in my opinion, with a superb set of plug-ins. However it is only available for Windows[61]. I am lucky that in my current job I have access to Paint.NET via the organisations software policy.

I have dipped in and out of using[Gimp](https://www.gimp.org/) for many years, and the recent set of add-ons to make Gimp present as much like Adobe as it can is, I think, a big boon for the software. Like a lot of users, I find that my _'muscle memory'_ for Paint.NET afflicts my use of any other image editor hanse my comments about the Adobe style presentation add-ions for Gimp 3.x.

But to bring this page to a close, I feel that the time has come to address my own 'muscle memory' issue and learn a new image editor. At teh moment, Affinity from Canva is getting rave reviews, and it works well. I particularly like that it combines three tools into one - page layout, vector graphics, and image editing all in one application, it reminds me of the _(very)_ old Windows Works package that combined word processing, spreadsheet, and a rudimentary database, and served me well for wrting my MBA siigenments back in the mid-90s. I digress....
